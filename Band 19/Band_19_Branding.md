# Branding

## Purpose

Define the complete visual and verbal identity of Deal Hunter AI
including logo usage, typography, iconography, color palette,
illustrations, app store assets, tone of voice, naming conventions, UI
consistency, accessibility, and brand governance.

## Scope

This document defines the strategic and technical requirements for this
domain.

-   Objectives
-   Architecture Impact
-   Functional Requirements
-   Non-Functional Requirements
-   Standards
-   Risks
-   Acceptance Criteria
-   Definition of Done
-   Future Extension Points

## Deliverables

-   Production-ready specification
-   Review checklist
-   Traceability to Band 01
